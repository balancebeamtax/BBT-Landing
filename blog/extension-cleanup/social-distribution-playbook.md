# Extension Cleanup Social Distribution Playbook

## Strategic recommendation

For the fastest lead generation, social traffic should usually go directly to `/extension-cleanup-review`, not through a blog article first.

Use the blog articles to build authority, SEO traffic, retargeting audiences, and organic education. Use the landing page when the social post or ad is asking the reader to take action.

## Best-practice funnel structure

### Funnel 1: Fast lead capture

Use when the post is aimed at business owners who already know their books are not ready.

Recommended channels:

- Instagram Story
- Instagram Reel caption link path
- Facebook post with direct CTA
- Facebook ad
- Retargeting ad

Destination:

- `/extension-cleanup-review`

Example CTA:

Request an Extension Cleanup Review

Why:

The objective is consultation bookings. Do not make a high-intent reader read a full article before asking for the appointment.

### Funnel 2: Education to conversion

Use when the post is informational and built around a practical article.

Recommended channels:

- Facebook organic link post
- LinkedIn educational post
- Email newsletter
- Blog hub internal links

Destination:

- Blog article first, then article CTA to `/extension-cleanup-review`

Example CTA:

Read the guide, then request a cleanup review if the issue matches your situation.

Why:

This builds trust and helps colder readers self-identify before converting.

### Funnel 3: Retargeting

Use when the reader visited a blog post or hub page but did not request a review.

Recommended channels:

- Facebook retargeting ad
- Instagram feed ad
- Instagram Story ad

Destination:

- `/extension-cleanup-review`

Example CTA:

Still working under an extension? Find out what is blocking the return.

Why:

The reader already consumed education. The next touch should ask for action.

## Facebook post best practice

Use two types of Facebook posts.

### Link post

Use when the goal is to promote a blog article.

Structure:

- Short plain-English hook.
- One practical takeaway.
- Link to the blog article.
- Blog article Open Graph image appears as the link preview.
- End copy mentions the cleanup review without making the post feel like an ad.

Example:

An extension gives more time to file. It does not clean up QuickBooks.

If your return is waiting on cleaner books, this guide explains what usually needs review before tax preparation can move forward.

Read the guide:

`/blog/quickbooks-cleanup-before-tax-filing`

If you already know the books are blocking the return, request a cleanup review:

`/extension-cleanup-review`

### Image post

Use when the goal is immediate lead generation.

Structure:

- Upload the feed image.
- Keep the caption short.
- Link directly to `/extension-cleanup-review`.
- Use UTM tracking.

Example:

Filed an extension because the books were not ready?

The deadline is still coming. The next step is to find out what needs cleanup before the return can be prepared.

Request an Extension Cleanup Review:

`/extension-cleanup-review?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=extension_cleanup_2026&utm_content=books_not_ready`

## Instagram best practice

Instagram is not ideal for blog click-through unless the account uses link stickers, bio links, or a link-in-bio page. Use Instagram primarily for direct-response visual hooks and send high-intent viewers to the landing page.

Recommended use:

- Feed carousel or single image: educational hook plus direct CTA.
- Story: vertical image with link sticker to `/extension-cleanup-review`.
- Reel: short educational script with caption CTA to the landing page.

Do not make the Instagram user go from Instagram to blog to landing page unless the post is purely educational.

## Paid social recommendation

If the objective is cash as soon as possible, paid or boosted social should not send cold traffic to a blog article as the primary path.

Recommended paid flow:

1. Ad creative uses the strongest pain-aware message.
2. Click goes to `/extension-cleanup-review`.
3. Landing page uses short intake and scheduling.
4. Retarget blog readers and landing page visitors who did not book.

Best first ad angles:

- Filed an extension because your books were not ready?
- September 15 is coming for many partnerships and S corporations.
- October 15 is coming for many Schedule C filers.
- QuickBooks report not ready for your preparer?

## Tracking requirements

Use UTMs by channel and content angle.

Recommended UTM pattern:

`/extension-cleanup-review?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=extension_cleanup_2026&utm_content=books_not_ready`

Track:

- Blog pageviews
- Blog-to-landing clicks
- Social-to-landing clicks
- Landing page form starts
- Landing page form completions
- Scheduled appointments
- Consultation show rate
- Closed cleanup engagements

## Bottom-line recommendation

Use the blog articles as authority and SEO assets. Use social graphics from the blog campaign, but route high-intent social traffic directly to `/extension-cleanup-review`.

The blog supports the funnel. The landing page closes the lead.
