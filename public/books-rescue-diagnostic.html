Common questions

Is this for me?

If you own a small business and your books are behind, messy, or unreconciled — yes. This diagnostic is designed for service-based businesses, freelancers, and LLCs with 1–20 employees.

What happens after I submit?

You get a scored result immediately. Based on your tier, a member of the Balance Beam Team will follow up within 1 business day to discuss next steps — no pressure, just a conversation.

Do I need my books ready before the call?

No. The call is diagnostic — we look at where things stand together. You don't need to prepare anything.

Is this tax advice?

No. This is a bookkeeping health diagnostic. We can discuss your books and cleanup options; specific tax advice requires a formal engagement.

How much does a Books Rescue engagement cost?

Cost depends on how far behind your books are and the complexity of your transactions. We'll give you a clear estimate on the call — no surprises.
